Theme proposal for Grades 1 to 10 school software

Square corners, clear colour.

Cobalt, turquoise and white are the three glazes of Multan’s kashi tile work. Kashi sets them on a ruled page with sharp corners and one-pixel lines. Colour is kept for two jobs only, the action you can take and the status of a record, so a fee table reads at a glance in light and in dark.

  • 0corner radius, everywhere
  • 1pxlines instead of shadows
  • 4pxspacing grid
  • AAcontrast in both modes

Layout rules

What every screen follows

Taken from your two references: hairline borders, capital micro-labels, compact toolbars, a filled table header and tinted badges. Corners are square on everything.

Corners0 on buttons, inputs, cards, badges, avatars, switches and chart bars
Lines1px. Structure comes from lines, never from shadows
ShadowsOnly on menus, dialogs and toasts
Controls28, 32 or 40px high. 32 is the default
Tables36px header, 40px rows (32 compact, 48 comfortable), 12px cell padding, numbers right-aligned
Sidebar208px wide, 32px items, 56px when collapsed
Page20px padding, 16px between blocks, cards padded 16px
Labels11px, capitals, letter-spaced, above the field
Focus2px ring with a 2px gap, on every control
IconsMaterial Symbols Sharp, 18px, the square-cornered set used on this page
RadioA diamond, so it never looks like a checkbox
CodesAdmission, roll, B-Form and voucher numbers in the mono face
UrduNastaliq, set larger with a line height of 2.1, right to left
Dates10 Oct 2026 in tables, never 10/10/26
