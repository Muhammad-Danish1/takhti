# Phase 16 · Documents and printing

**Goal.** Every document the school hands to someone looks right and prints right: the three-copy fee challan, receipts, report cards, certificates, ID cards, payslips, letters, and every report. Until now each "Print" button produced a placeholder; this phase replaces the placeholder with the real thing, without changing a single screen.

**Depends on.** Phases [7](phase-07-school-setup-and-people.md) to [15](phase-15-head-office-and-reports.md): the data behind every document exists.

**Modules.** All that produce a document; chiefly [M08](../10-modules/m08-certificates-id-cards.md), [M17](../10-modules/m17-exams-marks.md), [M18](../10-modules/m18-results-report-cards.md), [M21](../10-modules/m21-fees.md), [M28](../10-modules/m28-payroll.md), and the report view of [M42](../10-modules/m42-dashboards-reports.md).

## Read first

[13 · Jobs, notifications, files and documents](../00-foundation/13-notifications-jobs-and-files.md) (section 4), and the [document catalogue](../30-reference/document-catalogue.md), which lists every document with its size, data and variants. That catalogue is this phase's backlog.

## How a document is made

1. A service gathers the data and checks access (`requestDocument`, built in Phase 2).
2. A **print page** under `apps/web/app/print/<kind>/page.tsx` renders it as HTML, reached with a short-lived token, never by a signed-in session.
3. Headless Chromium (Puppeteer, in the worker) turns the page into a PDF with embedded fonts.
4. The PDF goes to the `generated` bucket and its row in `generated_documents` gets the file, a content hash and, where the catalogue says so, a verification code.
5. The screen that asked shows it in the **print view**: the document on a white page with a bar for Print, Download and, for a parent, Share.

One document renders inside the request. A batch (240 report cards, a month's challans) is a job that renders in groups of 25, merges them, and reports progress and any failures by name.

## Rules for every document

- **Black on white first.** Every document must be clear when printed on a monochrome laser printer; colour is a bonus, never the only carrier of meaning.
- **The school's identity, not Takhti's.** Letterhead with the school's logo, its name in English and Urdu, address, phone and registration numbers, from [M02](../10-modules/m02-organisation-branches.md). Takhti's name appears nowhere except a small "Generated by Takhti" where the school allows it.
- **Square corners and the Kashi type**, but with print sizes: body text 10 to 11 pt, nothing under 8 pt.
- **Urdu done properly**: Nastaliq, right-to-left blocks, larger size, generous line height; numbers and codes left-to-right. A bilingual document places English and Urdu side by side or in turn, never squeezed.
- **Numbers that matter are in a monospaced face and never wrap**: amounts, voucher and receipt numbers, admission and roll numbers.
- **Page breaks are designed**: a table header repeats; a row never splits; a signature block never sits alone on a page; "Page 2 of 3" on anything that can run over.
- **Every official document carries** its number, the date of issue, who issued it, and a QR code that opens the public verification page ([M08](../10-modules/m08-certificates-id-cards.md)) where the catalogue says so. A reprint says "Duplicate".
- **What is on screen is what prints.** The print view and the PDF come from the same page.
- **Paper sizes** are part of the document's definition: A4 portrait or landscape, A5, CR80 card (85.6 × 54 mm), 80 mm thermal roll, label sheets.

## Work packages

### 16.1 · The engine

- [ ] The PDF worker: Puppeteer in `packages/worker`, a browser kept warm, a page pool, a time limit per document, a restart after a number of renders or on a crash.
- [ ] Print tokens: single use, a 2-minute life, bound to one document request.
- [ ] `apps/web/app/print/layout.tsx`: print CSS, the bundled fonts, page size and margins from the document's definition, no application chrome.
- [ ] Building blocks in `apps/web/src/print/`: `Letterhead`, `DocumentTitle`, `PartyBlock` (student, family, staff, vendor), `KeyValueGrid`, `PrintTable` with repeating header and totals, `AmountInWords` (English and Urdu), `SignatureRow` (with signature images where set), `QrBlock`, `Watermark`, `PageFooter`, `CutLine`, `Barcode`.
- [ ] Batch rendering and merging; progress through `useJobProgress`; a failed item is named and can be retried alone.
- [ ] `PrintView` on the screen side: the PDF or, if the worker is unavailable, the print page itself in a frame with the browser's own print.
- [ ] Hardware notes page in the app's help: thermal receipt printers, card printers and label sheets that were tested.

### 16.2 · Fees and finance

The **three-copy challan** (bank copy, school copy, parent copy on one A4 sheet with cut lines; each with the challan number, the children and classes, the lines, "payable by" and "payable after" amounts, the bank's details and a barcode); the family challan; the **receipt** in A5 and 80 mm thermal; the family statement; defaulter notice; fee-paid certificate; refund voucher; cash session summary; and the finance set: journal voucher, trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, ledger, cash and bank books, purchase order, goods received note, payment voucher, vendor statement, tax certificates and schedules.

### 16.3 · Exams and results

Date sheet; **admit card** (several to a page); seating chart and attendance sheet per room; award list; invigilation roster; tabulation sheet; **report card in its five layouts** (standard, compact, primary descriptive, board style, cumulative), in English, Urdu or both, with the template options of [M18](../10-modules/m18-results-report-cards.md); result analysis; question paper (one or two columns, with equations, English, Urdu or both) and its answer key, with the watermark of who generated it.

### 16.4 · Certificates and ID cards — [M08](../10-modules/m08-certificates-id-cards.md)

The certificate types seeded in M08 (leaving, character, bonafide, provisional, fee, experience and the rest), each with its template variants; the template editor's live preview now showing the real document; **ID cards** front and back on CR80, eight to an A4 sheet for cutting or one per card for a card printer, for students and staff; the public verification page.

### 16.5 · Staff and payroll

Payslip (English, Urdu or both); salary register; bank advice letter; cash salary sheet with a signature column; salary certificate; appointment, confirmation, transfer, experience and relieving letters; final settlement statement; staff attendance register; leave form.

### 16.6 · Everything else

Admission form and offer letter; gate pass and visitor slip; class lists and attendance registers; timetables (section, teacher, room); lesson plan; library labels, cards and overdue notices; asset tags and stock sheets; transport route sheets; clinic slip and incident report; hostel pass; notices as circulars; event rosters and trip sheets; survey results.

### 16.7 · Reports

`ReportView`'s Print and "Export as PDF": any report in the library with letterhead, its filters, who ran it and when, repeating headers, totals and page numbers, portrait or landscape by its definition.

### 16.8 · Bulk printing

One screen pattern for "print many": choose the set (a class, a month, a selection), choose the layout, start, watch progress, download one merged file or a zip, see failures. Used for challans, report cards, admit cards, ID cards and payslips.

## Journeys to automate

| Journey | Steps |
|---|---|
| A challan | Generate a family's challan → three copies with identical figures → the "payable after" amount includes the fine rule → the barcode decodes to the challan number |
| A receipt on thermal | Collect a fee → the 80 mm receipt has no content cut off at 72 mm printable width |
| A class's report cards | Generate 40 cards → progress → one merged PDF of 40 pages in roll order → a withheld student has no card |
| Urdu | An Urdu report card renders in Nastaliq with correct right-to-left tables and left-to-right numbers |
| Verify | Scan a leaving certificate's QR code → the public page confirms it; alter the code → "not found" |
| Duplicate | Reprint a receipt → it says "Duplicate" |
| Failure | With the PDF worker stopped, the print view still prints from the browser; a batch reports the worker is unavailable and can be resumed |
| A long statement | A family statement of four pages repeats its header and says "Page 3 of 4" |

## Exit checks

1. Every document in the [document catalogue](../30-reference/document-catalogue.md) renders from seeded data; a test renders each one and compares it with a stored reference image (visual comparison at a small tolerance).
2. Each document has been **printed on paper** on a monochrome laser printer and checked by a person against a checklist: margins, cut lines, legibility of the smallest text, Urdu, signatures. The thermal receipt and the ID card are printed on their own hardware or the nearest available.
3. A batch of 500 challans completes in under five minutes on the development machine and uses bounded memory.
4. No print page is reachable without a valid token; a token works once.
5. Every "Print" button in the product now produces its real document; a crawl finds no placeholder.
6. `pnpm check`, `pnpm test:db`, `pnpm test:e2e`.
